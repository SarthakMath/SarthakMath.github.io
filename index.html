About

Before Gandhinagar I spent five years at IISER Bhopal, where I took a BS–MS with a major in mathematics and wrote a master’s thesis on composition operators on the Hardy space.

My interests are the numerical analysis of PDEs, applied mathematics, and physics-informed neural networks. I write code in Julia, Python, C and Mathematica, and I like building small interactive tools that let you watch a numerical method work.
